# Evidence and capability limits

Use the host's working search/browser/maps; do not require a particular vendor, API key, account, or another skill. Do not copy credentials into the skill or its artifacts.

For a decision-critical claim, prefer dated first-party operating notices and official booking/transport pages. Use maps for locations and route estimates; recent local reviews can inform queues, crowding and experience fit but do not establish admission rules. Search snippets are discovery, not verification. Open the source where possible. Avoid citing a homepage for a precise rule it does not contain.

Minimal ledger example:

| Claim | Visit date | Source | Checked at | Status / consequence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Museum last entry 16:00 | YYYY-MM-DD | exact official URL | YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM UTC+08:00 | verified published rule; slot inventory unknown |
| Hotel to gate 45–60 min | YYYY-MM-DD | route provider + endpoints | timestamp | estimated; includes walking/wait, add entry queue separately |

`verified` means the cited source establishes this exact claim, not that an entire stop is guaranteed. Historical/climatological weather is not a forecast. A sold-out date is not equivalent to a closed attraction.

For an inaccessible fact, try a relevant alternative official page/provider or another specific query. Stop once remaining attempts repeat the same evidence or require an unavailable login/tool; record the limitation rather than searching indefinitely. Exact-date tickets not yet released must be labeled not released/unknown, with the announced release rule if known.

With no live tools, use user-supplied evidence or clearly labeled static assumptions. Provide a provisional structure, preserve uncertainty, and do not describe it as a verified current itinerary. For official entry/visa requirements or other high-stakes decisions, link the appropriate authority and avoid presenting inference as a ruling. No live booking or payment is implied by planning.
